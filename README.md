<h2>Projects</h2>

<h3><a href="https://github.com/Anurag6905/bharat_bricks">Fraud Lens</a></h3>
<p><sub>XGBoost · Isolation Forest · RAG · Llama 3.3 70B · Databricks · Streamlit</sub></p>
<p>
Reviewing UPI fraud cases was slow and fully manual. Fraud Lens flags risky transactions with an ML pipeline, then uses a RAG agent over 500+ regulatory documents to explain why each one was flagged. It reached <b>85% accuracy</b> and cut review time by <b>55%</b>.
</p>

<h3><a href="https://github.com/Anurag6905/AI-System-for-NGO-finance-and-Analytics">FinEdge AI Agent</a></h3>
<p><sub>RAG · open-source LLMs · NLP</sub></p>
<p>
Magic Bus Foundation's team spent hours every week on finance reports. This agent lets them ask finance questions in plain English, and it runs fully on-premise so no data leaves the organisation. It answers with <b>83% accuracy</b>, saves about <b>15 hours a week</b>, and won <b>3rd prize out of 500+</b> at the FinEdge Hackathon.
</p>

<h3><a href="https://github.com/Anurag6905/HospiGo">HospiGo</a></h3>
<p><sub>FastAPI · React · Leaflet</sub></p>
<p>
Patients had no easy way to see which nearby hospitals had free beds or OPD slots. HospiGo puts them on one live map with booking and separate logins for patients and hospitals. It tracks <b>500+ beds</b> and halved the time it takes to book one.
</p>

<h3>Deepfake Detector</h3>
<p><sub>PyTorch · ResNet-18 · Qdrant</sub></p>
<p>
Most deepfake detectors give you a yes or no and nothing else. This one classifies the video, then searches 120K embeddings to show the closest known fakes as evidence. It runs at <b>84% accuracy</b> in about <b>60 ms per query</b>, and made the Convolve semi-finals.
</p>

<h3>AutoDevX</h3>
<p><sub>LLM agents · CI/CD · Python</sub></p>
<p>
Manual code review kept missing UX bugs. AutoDevX runs 12 AI agents that use the app like real users inside the CI/CD pipeline and patch what they can. It caught <b>150+ critical issues</b> and cut review time by <b>40%</b>.
</p>
